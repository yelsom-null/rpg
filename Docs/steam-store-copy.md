## Long description

**The mountain pays well. The mountain keeps what it buries.**

Deepseam is a short-session, push-your-luck mining game. Below the camp at
Bracken Cross runs a ten-band mine — every band richer, tighter, and closer
to coming down on your head.

**DIG.** Click to mine. Listen for the crack and time your strike on the weak
point for pristine ore. Tap the walls to hear which rocks ring full. Follow
the seams.

**READ THE ROCK.** Every swing shakes the gallery. Quiet stone becomes
creaking timbers becomes racing cracks becomes a roof coming down. Shore it
with timber, or take what you've got and climb.

**CASH OUT — OR LOSE THE BAG.** Your haul is only safe on the surface. Die
underground and everything you carry stays there. Gold, skills, the pickaxe
on your belt, and whatever you stashed in the Vault survive to fund the next
dive.

**FORGE UP.** Smelt graded ore into graded bars. Work the anvil — pump the
bellows, hammer in the orange, quench the instant it's done — for a pickaxe
that swings faster and strikes truer. Fill caravan orders for a premium.

**FIND THE HEART.** Band ten holds the Heart of the Mountain. Mine it and
you've won — then keep digging the endless deeps, or roll a new mountain.
Every save is a fresh seed. No two mountains dig the same.

- 10–20 minute runs, first win in a couple of hours
- One mechanic, polished: mine under pressure, upgrade between dives
- Deterministic authoritative sim — deaths are always your fault
- Cozy low-poly camp, claustrophobic low-poly deeps
- No meta-currency soup: ore → gold → gear, that's the whole economy
